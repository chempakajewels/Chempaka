From bullion to boutique

Chempaka was born from a single vision: to bridge traditional Asian goldsmithing and modern, minimalist luxury. We believed 916 and 999 gold deserved to be celebrated in contemporary design, not only kept in safes and worn on wedding days.

The artisan process

Every piece begins as raw bullion. We control the alloying in-house so our 916 gold keeps its signature, radiant warmth, and every piece is hand-finished so the gold catches the light with a soft glow rather than a machine-made shine.
